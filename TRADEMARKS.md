# Names and branding

The code is GPL-3.0. The name is not. This is the plain English version of the
trademark term in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Not licensed

- **ODG**
- **ODG Operations**
- **Operator Development Group**
- **Archduke**
- the ODG logo and insignia, including the patches and unit markings in the pack

The GPL grants you the code and says nothing about the name. GPL-3.0 section
7(e) lets a copyright holder state that explicitly, which is what we do.

## You may

- Fork the repository and modify it.
- Say your work is based on, derived from, or a fork of ODG Operations, and link
  back here.
- Keep our copyright notices in place. Section 7(b) requires it.

## You may not

- Name your fork ODG anything, or a name close enough to be confused with it.
- Use the ODG logo, insignia or patches for your work.
- Present your build as an official ODG Operations release.
- Use our names to promote your version or imply we endorse it.

## Reporting a reupload

Open an issue here or reach us through the links in the README.

Two different things go wrong in a reupload, so say which one you found:

1. They shipped binaries built from this code without publishing their source
   under the GPL. The download itself is the evidence.
2. They called it ODG.
